# construction

> status: researched, unseeded. every canonical here is a candidate until a real image blesses it.

where is the camera?

that is the only question this folder answers. not what the subject is, not how it is rendered, not how deep the scene is. where is the implied viewer standing, and what did that position do to the shapes.

## why this is separate from render

two images can share a render type (block_fill) and differ completely in construction (one a flat elevation, one a worm's eye view). conflating them is what makes a style transfer tilt the horizon when the user only asked for chunkier marks. construction is one of the orthogonal dial clusters, and it must be independently controllable.

## the four axes

| axis | question |
|---|---|
| projective_system | what happens to parallel lines? |
| eye_level | where does the horizon sit relative to the subject? |
| convergence_strength | how disciplined are the vanishing lines? |
| foreshortening | how squashed are the shapes facing the camera? |

definitions in `axes.md`. candidate canonicals in `canonical_tags.md`, all unseeded. synonyms in `synonyms.md`, all anticipated.

## open questions

- camera roll (dutch angle) exists in the world but has no axis here yet. it is a real camera property, users say it, but no seed image in the session needed it. noted as a gap, not added.
- oblique/axonometric projections (isometric, cabinet, military) collapse into `flat_parallel` for now. if real usage keeps distinguishing "isometric" from "storybook flat", the axis splits. synonyms first, canonicals later.
