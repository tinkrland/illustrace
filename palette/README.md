# palette

> status: researched, unseeded, and deliberately built last. color is the domain most prone to confidently wrong taxonomies.

what is the color doing?

not how the marks were made (render), not where the camera is (construction), not what depth does (spatial). what organizational statement does the color make.

## the conflation trap this folder exists to kill

"pastel" is a render canonical (pastel_open: loose dry-medium marks with intentional white) and also a palette mood (high-key, low-saturation, warm). "muted" can mean desaturated hues or a fog treatment (spatial atmospheric_cue). every vocabulary-level failure in style transfer traces to some version of this trap: one word doing two domain jobs. the wrangler must split the word into its domains before anything touches the engine.

## the four axes

| axis | question |
|---|---|
| value_structure | how is light/dark organized, and how many values carry the design? |
| hue_scheme | what is the relationship between the hues? |
| saturation_mode | how loud is the color, and where? |
| temperature_bias | does the image lean warm, cool, or split? |

definitions in `axes.md`. candidates in `canonical_tags.md`, all unseeded. synonyms all anticipated, and this folder's `_ambiguous` list is expected to be the longest in the branch.
