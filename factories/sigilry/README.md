# sigilry (seal and emblem factory)

the marks-of-authority factory: emblems, wax seals, rubber stamps, stamp marks, monograms, crests, heraldic-adjacent forms. the entire family of "this thing is official" marks that people put on packaging, letters, certificates, packaging tape, beer labels, and the back of envelopes.

## what it makes

- wax seals: blob-edge seal bodies (inkora is a neighbor), impression depth, sheen, ribbon slots
- rubber stamps and stamp marks: ink coverage, edge wear, rotation jitter, the imperfect-ink look, as parameters
- monograms: interlocking letterforms from fontasy glyphs with arrangement parameters
- crests and emblems: shield and cartouche bases, heraldic arrangement rules, ornamental frames (filigree is the detail supplier)

## the editable-preset contract

everything stays a layered vector with per-layer overrides: the seal body, the impressed mark inside it, the texture of the wax, the ink pattern of the stamp. a stamp mark is source mark + ink recipe layer, so restyling the source restyles the stamp.

## on the name

sigilry, coined the way jewelry is coined, sigil + -ry. provisional; alternates considered: sealmark, crestforge, waxworks. rename is a one-line change while the folder is still a readme.

## studio role

the composer's ceremony layer: seal the wordmark, stamp the packaging, monogram the crest. pairs naturally with analogistry (stamps want ink) and weaverly (stitched seals are a beloved pinterest genre).
