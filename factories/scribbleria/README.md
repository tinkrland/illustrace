# scribbleria (doodle factory)

the loose-line factory. scribbled doodles: the imperfect, hand-drawn, slightly-off-model marks that read as personality precisely because they're not clean. this is the opposite instinct from inkora (illustrace's clean parametric shape factory): where inkora sculpts a precise outline, scribbleria's whole point is a controlled, repeatable amount of looseness, wobble, double-lines, overshoot, the visible evidence of a hand.

## what it makes

- doodle marks: stars, hearts, arrows, scribbly fills, the decorative vocabulary that surrounds stickers and captions
- loose-line treatments over any base shape: a clean silhouette from another factory can be re-inked in scribbleria's hand, wobble and overshoot as parameters, not a filter guess
- doodled humanoid bases: rough, sketchy figure scaffolding, the starting material characraft's picrew-style customizer builds on top of

## the parameter surface

looseness is not "add noise," it's a specific small vocabulary: line wobble (frequency and amplitude of hand-tremor deviation from the ideal path), overshoot (how far a stroke runs past its intended endpoint before lifting), double-strike (a second, offset pass suggesting a correction stroke), pressure variance (line weight breathing along its length), corner rounding-off. each is a slider, each is deterministic once seeded.

## the editable-preset contract

the doodled look is a recipe layer over a real vector path, not a hand-jitter shader baked into a raster. the underlying path stays clean and editable; scribbleria's parameters describe how to re-ink it. reopen the preset, get the exact same wobble.

## relationship to charatrace

scribbleria is the likely source of the doodled humanoid base body that [characraft](/characraft) starts from: "doodled type of character" needs a linework hand, and that hand is this factory's whole job. it's also a general decorative-doodle supplier for stickers, captions, and margins wherever charatrace or illustrace wants a looser mark than their parametric factories produce.
